Status & Warning Indicators Guide

Understand what warning badges in the Transactions list and toolbar represent:

None / (Deleted) Missing Subcategory

When a subcategory is deleted or unassigned, transactions remain completely intact with all financial records preserved. You can click Edit (pencil) on the transaction to assign a new subcategory.

Category (Deleted) Deleted Category

When a category is deleted, it is moved to the Recycle Bin. You can restore the category from Settings > Recycle Bin or edit affected transactions to reassign their category.

X Warnings Toolbar Warning Counter

Beside the date picker in Transactions, this badge alerts you whenever transactions need subcategory review. Click it to instantly filter and review those transactions.
